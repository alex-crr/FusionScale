1:1 Scale

Zooms the viewport so your design shows at true physical size on your screen, after a one-time ruler calibration.

How to use

  1. Design workspace, Solid tab, Inspect panel: click 1:1 Scale.
  2. First run: hold a ruler against the reference line on screen, enter the measured length in millimetres, and click OK.
  3. Later runs zoom to 1:1 straight away.
  4. Use Recalibrate Scale after a change of monitor, resolution or Windows display scaling.

Notes

  • True scale is exact only for faces square to the camera. The view is switched to orthographic.
  • The calibration applies to one screen at a time. Calibrate on the monitor where you check sizes.
  • Only the camera changes, never the design.
